Alec Xu

Contact: alecx [at] umich [dot] edu

Hello! I'm currently a PhD student at the University of Michigan, where I'm fortunate to be advised by Qing Qu as part of the DeepThink Lab. I also collaborate closely with Laura Balzano. In Summer 2026, I was a machine learning intern at Apple under Baris Sen and Henning Tjaden. Previously, I earned my MSE and BSE degrees also at the University of Michigan, where I worked with Jeff Fessler.

I have worked on a wide variety of problems. My PhD work focuses on theoretical foundations of deep learning and generative AI models, particularly via optimization and statistics. In my past internships, I've worked on on-device video object segmentation, 3D image segmentation for brain mapping, and object detection for multi-channel signal detection & classification.

I am currently looking for full-time research or applied scientist positions!
